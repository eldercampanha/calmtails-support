A few things to know

Getting started with CalmTails.

How do I get the app?

The free iPhone app has been released in Canada. Android still requires testing access while we prepare its public release. See download links and availability. If you have a Premium Beta invitation, install first and then reopen your personal invitation link to activate it.

Does a Beta invitation share my dog’s data?

No. Activating Premium Beta and granting veterinary access are separate choices. In the app, open your dog’s profile, choose Dogs & Sharing, and use Add a veterinarian to create a sharing link. Send that link to your participating vet; they review and accept access in the portal.

Can any veterinarian sign up?

The portal is currently invitation-only. Vets with an available veterinarian invitation allowance can invite a colleague. Each account’s available invitations are shown in Your invitations.

What happens when Premium Beta ends?

Access returns to the free plan unless another active entitlement applies. There is no automatic paid subscription. Your saved records remain accessible; Premium tools require active access.

How can I send feedback?

Use Settings → Feedback in the app, reply to the person who invited you, or email us. For a bug, tell us what you were doing and what you expected to happen. Never include your password or invitation link.
