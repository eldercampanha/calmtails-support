Written guide for dog owners

Record a daily observation
  1. Open Daily Log and choose a day in the calendar.
  2. Choose Separation Anxiety or Reactivity, then the state you observed.
  3. Add a short note and any observed signs that help explain what happened.
  4. Tap Save. Reopen that day to review the entry.

You’re done when: the observation appears in that day’s behavior log.

Accept your Premium Beta invitation
  1. If needed, install CalmTails using the installation link provided by your inviter.
  2. Reopen the same personal invitation link on your phone after installation.
  3. If you’re new, create an account. If you already have an account, sign in. If you’re already signed in, check that it’s the account you want to use.
  4. Continue through the invitation screen to activate access.
  5. Open Settings to check your Premium Beta status and expiry date.

You’re done when: Settings shows complimentary Premium Beta access.

No payment method or automatic paid subscription. Beta access does not automatically share your records.

Installation and invitation troubleshooting

Share your dog’s records with your vet
  1. Make sure your Premium Beta access is active and your veterinarian is participating in the portal.
  2. Open your dog’s profile, then Dogs & Sharing.
  3. Choose Add a veterinarian and follow the on-screen steps for the intended vet.
  4. Review the access you grant. You can remove it later from the sharing controls.

What this does: lets your selected veterinary professional review your dog’s shared records. They cannot edit your journal entries.

The veterinary invitation and owner Premium Beta invitation are different. Your vet joins the portal; you grant access to your dog.

Written guide for veterinary professionals

Accept a veterinarian invitation
  1. Open the personal veterinarian invitation link sent to you.
  2. Sign in with your portal account, or create one through the invitation flow.
  3. Complete acceptance and open the portal.

Next: owners need to share their dog’s records with you before you can review them. Joining the portal alone does not reveal patient records.

Invite an owner to Premium Beta
  1. Open Your invitations and check your remaining owner allowance.
  2. Choose Create owner invitation and confirm.
  3. Copy or share the personal link before closing the dialog. It is only shown once.
  4. Send the link privately to its intended recipient, along with their installation instructions.
  5. Point them to the owner activation steps. They must grant dog-sharing access separately.

What the owner receives: an invitation for 90 days of complimentary Premium Beta, with no automatic paid billing.

Invite a veterinary colleague
  1. Open Your invitations and check whether you have a veterinarian invitation available.
  2. If available, create a veterinarian invitation and share its personal link privately with your colleague.
  3. Your colleague opens the link, signs in or creates an account, and accepts portal access.

Check the allowance: not every vet can invite colleagues. An invited vet may have owner invitations only. The portal shows each allowance separately.

If no veterinarian invitations are available, contact your inviter. An owner invitation cannot be used to join the portal.

Review shared records and use notes
  1. Choose a dog whose owner has granted you access.
  2. Open Logs to review observations. Search or use the date filters to focus on the period you need.
  3. Use the other record views for behavior and medication context.
  4. Open Notes to review household notes and send a follow-up update or message. You can delete notes you authored.

Keep in mind: Vet Notes is experimental. You can review shared journal records, but cannot edit the owner’s entries.

New phone installation?

Installing the app does not activate the invitation automatically. Reopen the same invitation link after installation. Invitation activation is explained with text in the video; the app footage shows an already-active Premium Beta account.

The link shown in the recording is a blurred demo link. Use the personal link sent by your inviter.
