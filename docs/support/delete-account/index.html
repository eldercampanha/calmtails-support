Delete your Calm Tails account

You can request deletion even if you no longer have the app. Email us from the address you use to sign in to Calm Tails. If you can no longer access that address, include it in your request so we can follow up to verify ownership. Never send your password.

Email Calm Tails to request account deletion

Delete from the app

Sign in, open Settings → Delete account, and confirm with your password.

What happens to your data

Deleting your account removes your sign-in and the records associated with it, including dogs you own alone and their journal records. Dogs with another owner stay with that owner, along with the other owner's records. Copies you exported or shared with other services are managed separately.
