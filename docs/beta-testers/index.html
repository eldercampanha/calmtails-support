Your owner invitation

A warm welcome
to Premium Beta.

Your personal invitation gives you 90 days to try the Premium tools, free. There’s no automatic paid subscription. The welcome screen and Settings show your access details.

Keep your invitation private: it is intended for one recipient.

  1. Install CalmTails

    Use the installation link provided by your inviter. We’re rolling out access gradually.

  2. Reopen the same invitation link

    Return to the message containing your personal Premium Beta link and open it on your phone after installation.

  3. Use your CalmTails account

    Sign in or create an account. If you’re already signed in, check that it’s the account you want to use before accepting.

  4. Activate and explore

    Continue through the invitation screen. Then try a daily entry, a structured walk, or an appointment report.

  5. Choose what to share

    Beta access alone does not share your records. To add a participating veterinarian, open your dog’s profile → Dogs & Sharing. You can remove their access later.

Your veterinary invitation

A small pilot.
A useful conversation.

The Early Access portal is invitation-only. Use the veterinarian link sent to you to create your account or sign in.

Open the vet portal

A portal account does not automatically give access to any dog’s records. The owner must grant sharing permission.

Inviting owners to Premium Beta

Open Your invitations, choose Create owner invitation, and confirm. Copy or share the link privately with its intended recipient before closing the dialog. The link is only shown once.

Inviting a colleague

If your account has veterinarian invitations available, create a veterinarian invitation from the same page. Owner and veterinarian invitations serve different purposes and cannot be used interchangeably.

Check your own allowance

The portal shows your remaining invitations. Allowances vary during the pilot; a newly invited vet may have owner invitations without permission to invite other vets.

Reviewing shared records

Select a shared dog to review behavior, medication and other context. Use Logs to search or filter by date. Vet Notes supports follow-up updates and messages; authors can delete their own notes in the portal.

If you get stuck

A few common questions.

I installed the app, but Beta isn’t active.

Reopen your original invitation link and finish the activation flow. Check that you’re signed into the intended account. Installing the app on its own does not activate an invitation.

My invitation is expired, revoked, or already used.

Contact the person who invited you. They can check the invitation and, if appropriate and within their allowance, issue a replacement. A used invitation cannot activate another account.

I closed the link before copying it.

In Your invitations, revoke the pending invitation and create a replacement if your allowance permits. The original link is not shown again.

Will I be charged after the Beta?

No. Complimentary Beta access does not automatically become a paid subscription. Access may expire or be withdrawn; your saved records remain accessible.

Where should I send feedback?

In the app, open Settings → Feedback. You can also reply directly to your inviter or email CalmTails. Tell us what worked, what was confusing, and what you expected. Please leave out passwords, invitation links, and other people’s private information.

A tool for your care conversations

CalmTails records what owners observe. It does not provide a diagnosis, veterinary consultations, or medical advice. Contact your veterinary team for care decisions.
