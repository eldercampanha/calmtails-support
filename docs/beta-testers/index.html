For dog owners · Premium Beta

Email, then web, then app.

Use your phone. You’ll finish when the app says Welcome to Premium Beta and shows your expiry date.

  1. Email or messageOpen your personal invitation
  2. CalmTails websiteTap “Open CalmTails and accept”
  3. CalmTails appSign in and activate Premium Beta
  1. EMAIL OR MESSAGE · ON YOUR PHONE

    Find the two different links

    Your inviter should send an installation link and a personal Premium Beta invitation link. The installation link gets the app onto your phone. The personal invitation unlocks Beta for your CalmTails account.

    App not installed? Follow the installation instructions first. Then return to the original email or message and open the personal invitation. If no installation instructions were included, open our download page for current iPhone and Android availability.

  2. WEBSITE · YOUR PHONE’S BROWSER

    Open the personal invitation, then open the app

    The link opens a page on portal.calmtails.ca titled A personal invitation for you and your dog. Check the invitation details and tap Open CalmTails and accept.

    If your phone asks whether to open CalmTails, choose Open. Seeing this web page alone has not activated Beta.

    Installed the app after opening the link? Go back to the same email or message and reopen the same personal invitation. Installation does not carry the invitation into the app automatically.
  3. APP · YOUR CALMTAILS ACCOUNT

    Choose how to continue

    No account yet

    Tap Create my account and complete signup in the app.

    Already have an account

    Tap I already have an account and sign in.

    Already signed in

    Check the email shown. Use Use a different account if it is not the account you want.

    Your invitation is kept while you sign in. If the invitation screen does not return, reopen the original personal link.

  4. EMAIL, THEN BACK TO THE APP · ONLY IF ASKED

    Confirm a new account if prompted

    You may receive a separate account-confirmation email. Use its confirmation link, then return to CalmTails and sign in.

    This is different from your invitation email: confirming your email verifies the account. You still need to activate Premium Beta in the next step.

  5. APP · FINAL ACTIVATION

    Tap “Activate my Premium Beta”

    If shown, tap Review terms to continue and review the Terms & Privacy. Return to the invitation and tap Activate my Premium Beta.

    Success: you see Welcome to Premium Beta, an expiry date, and Start exploring. Settings also shows your Beta access.

    You have 90 days of complimentary access from activation. No payment method or automatic paid subscription.

Want your vet to see your dog’s records? That needs a separate sharing invitation. Follow the owner-to-vet sharing steps.

For veterinary professionals · Portal Early Access

Email, then web.
No app installation required.

Use the veterinarian invitation sent to you. This creates portal access; it does not grant access to a particular dog.

  1. Email or messageOpen your vet invitation
  2. Web portalSign in or create your account
  3. Web portalActivate Early Access and finish your profile
  1. EMAIL OR MESSAGE

    Open your personal veterinarian invitation

    The personal link opens the Early Access invitation page on portal.calmtails.ca. An owner Premium Beta invitation cannot be used to join the veterinary portal.

  2. WEBSITE · SIGN IN OR SIGN UP

    Use your portal account

    Choose I already have an account or Create my account. If you’re already signed in, select Continue with my account and check the account email.

    If signup asks you to confirm your email, open that separate confirmation email, then return to the portal and sign in. Reopen the original invitation if needed.

  3. WEBSITE · ACTIVATE

    Activate your Early Access

    Review Terms & Privacy if prompted, then choose Activate my Early Access.

    Success: the page says Welcome to the founding group. Choose Explore the portal and complete your professional profile if prompted.

  4. WEBSITE · AFTER YOU JOIN

    Receive shared records and check your invitations

    Owners must send you a separate dog-record sharing invitation before you can review their dog. See that flow below.

    Open Your invitations to see your owner and veterinarian allowances. A newly invited vet may have owner invitations without permission to invite more vets.

A separate invitation · Dog-record sharing

Owner’s app, then vet’s inbox,
then vet’s portal.

Accepting Premium Beta or joining the portal does not share any dog’s records. The owner starts this separate flow when they want a participating vet to have access.

  1. Owner’s appCreate a dog-record access invitation
  2. Email or messageOwner sends the link to their vet
  3. Vet’s web portalVet reviews the dog and accepts access
  1. OWNER · IN THE APP

    Create and send the sharing invitation

    With active Premium access, open your dog’s profile → Dogs & Sharing → Add a veterinarian. Choose Share now and send the generated link and code privately to the intended participating veterinarian.

    Use the veterinary access section. Create 5-minute share code is for household sharing, not this veterinary access flow.

  2. VET · EMAIL OR MESSAGE, THEN WEB

    Open the dog’s access link

    The vet opens the link sent by the owner, signs in to their invited portal account, and completes their profile if prompted. They review the page titled View [dog’s name]’s CalmTails records, then choose Accept access.

    A dog-record sharing link does not replace a veterinarian’s Early Access invitation. Vets who have not joined the portal need that invitation first.

  3. VET · WEB PORTAL

    Review the shared dog

    Success: the dog appears in the vet’s shared records. The vet can review the records; the owner can remove access later.

If you get stuck

A few common questions.

I installed the app, but Beta isn’t active.

Reopen the personal invitation from your original email or message. On the website, tap Open CalmTails and accept. In the app, sign in and choose Activate my Premium Beta. Installing the app or confirming your email is not the activation step.

I opened the invitation on my computer.

For an owner Premium Beta invitation, open the same personal link on the phone where CalmTails is installed. For a veterinarian invitation, you can continue in your computer’s browser.

My invitation is expired, revoked, or already used.

Contact the person who invited you. They can check the invitation and, if appropriate and within their allowance, issue a replacement. A used invitation cannot activate another account.

I closed the link before copying it.

In Your invitations, revoke the pending invitation and create a replacement if your allowance permits. The original link is not shown again.

Will I be charged after the Beta?

No. Complimentary Beta access does not automatically become a paid subscription. Access may expire or be withdrawn; your saved records remain accessible.

Where should I send feedback?

In the app, open Settings → Feedback. You can also reply directly to your inviter or email CalmTails. Tell us what worked, what was confusing, and what you expected. Please leave out passwords, invitation links, and other people’s private information.

A tool for your care conversations

CalmTails records what owners observe. It does not provide a diagnosis, veterinary consultations, or medical advice. Contact your veterinary team for care decisions.
