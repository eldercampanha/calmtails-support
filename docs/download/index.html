Get CalmTails
on your phone.

Start with the free journal. If you have a Premium Beta invitation, install the app first, then return to your personal invitation link.

iPhone · Canadian App Store

The free iPhone app

CalmTails was released for Canada on November 19. The listing may take a little time to appear after release.

Open the App Store

If the store says it is unavailable, check that your App Store region is Canada and try again later. An invitation does not change your store region.

Android · Testing access for now

Android is not public yet

The free Android release has been submitted to Google Play for review. Until it is approved and published, you need a separate Google Play testing invitation to install.

Ask your inviter for the testing link and use the Google account enrolled in the test. A Premium Beta link alone does not grant Google Play testing access.

Ask for Android testing help

Have a Premium Beta invitation?

Install first. Then come back.

  1. Install CalmTails using the option for your phone above.
  2. Return to the email or message containing your personal invitation and open that same link.
  3. Tap Open CalmTails and accept, sign in or create your account, then tap Activate my Premium Beta.

You’re finished when the app says Welcome to Premium Beta and shows your expiry date. Installing the app alone does not activate Beta. No payment method or automatic paid subscription.
